Why is the Ethiopian year different?

The Ethiopian calendar is based on the Alexandrian or Coptic calendar. It is typically 7 to 8 years behind the Gregorian calendar. This is because the Ethiopian calendar has an extra 13th month (Pagume) and calculates the birth of Jesus Christ differently.
