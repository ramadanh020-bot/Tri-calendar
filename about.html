About Tri-Calendar Tool

Last updated: January 2026

Tri-Calendar Tool is a free, browser-based utility that converts between three major calendar systems used across Ethiopia, the Middle East, and the world: the Ethiopian calendar, the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, and the Gregorian calendar.

Why We Built This

Ethiopia is one of the few countries that uses its own calendar system alongside the Gregorian one. Meanwhile, millions of Muslims in Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa also rely on the Hijri calendar for religious observance. Despite this overlap, there was no single free web tool that handled all three calendars together — with conversion, day counting, Ethiopian time, and a printable view.

We built Tri-Calendar Tool to fill that gap. It's fast, works on any device, and requires no signup.

What It Does

  • Tri-calendar view: See today's date in Ethiopian, Hijri, and Gregorian formats side by side.
  • Date converter: Convert from any of the three calendars into the other two.
  • Day counter: Calculate the number of days between two dates, with equivalents in all three calendars.
  • Ethiopian hour calculator: Convert between Ethiopian time (which starts at dawn) and standard 24-hour time.
  • World clock: Check the current time in 12 major cities, including Addis Ababa, Mecca, Cairo, and Dubai.
  • Printable tri-calendar: Generate a printable month view showing all three calendars, with an option to make any calendar the "priority" (larger, bold, visually dominant).

How the Conversion Works

All conversions use the Julian Day Number (JDN) as a universal bridge. Each calendar is first converted to its JDN, and then the JDN is converted into the target calendar. This ensures mathematical consistency across all three systems.

Hijri Accuracy Note

Hijri dates in this tool are astronomical estimates based on the Umm al-Qura calendar. Actual Hijri dates may differ by ±1 day depending on local moon sighting. For religious observances, please consult your local mosque or authority.

Privacy

All calculations run entirely in your browser. No personal data is transmitted, stored, or shared. See our Privacy Policy for details.
